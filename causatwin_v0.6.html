Headline Results

💡 6 Counter-Intuitive Findings

  1. Solo > Multi-Agent in all 3 groups — Group A +34.4pp (68.4% vs 34.0%), Group B +17.4pp (61.7% vs 50.0%), Group C +35.8pp (81.6% vs 45.8%)
  2. Novelty Halo Reversal — Multi-Agent achieves 56.5% on HIGH-novelty but only 33.9% on LOW-novelty; Solo is approximately flat across tiers
  3. Recall(Reject) < 30% for Multi-Agent, but Solo-C reaches 88.9% (25/28 correct Reject identifications)
  4. Frozen CT.gov is double-edged — helps Multi-Agent (+15.2pp) but actually hurts Solo (-6.7pp)
  5. BEA/BCA disagreement inverted — smallest on HIGH-novelty (3.44), largest on LOW-novelty (5.94)
  6. F16–F25 Non-novel failed cases — Multi-Agent 3/10 correct; Solo 7/7 correct. Mechanism: negative-precedent blindness
